Our Story

Welcome to Copper Star Coffee

Served up in a re-purposed service station, Copper Star Coffee is a locally owned, independent coffee shop & café featuring hot and cold coffees, teas, hand-blended drinks, espresso, cappuccino, smoothies and an assortment of Italian sodas.
